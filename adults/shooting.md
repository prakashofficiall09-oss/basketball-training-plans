# Adults Shooting Drill

## Purpose
Improve shooting efficiency under fatigue and game-like pacing.

## Equipment Needed
- Basketballs
- Hoop
- Spot markers
- Timer

## Instructions
1. Select 5 perimeter and mid-range shooting spots.
2. Shoot in sequence with quick movement between spots.
3. Record makes in a timed round (e.g., 2 minutes).
4. Rest briefly and repeat multiple rounds.

## Coaching Tips
- Maintain form even when fatigued.
- Prepare feet before receiving or gathering the ball.
- Track results to monitor consistency over time.
