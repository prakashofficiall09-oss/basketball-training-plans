# Mirror Slides Drill

**Purpose:** Build defensive stamina, lateral quickness, and balance.

**Equipment Needed:** Cones (optional).

**Instructions:**
1. Partners face each other in a defensive stance.
2. The leader slides left/right; the defender mirrors the movement.
3. Work in 30–45 second intervals, then switch roles.

**Coaching Tips:** Stay low, stay wide, and keep hands active throughout.
