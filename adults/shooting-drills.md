# Form Shooting Drill

**Purpose:** Reinforce consistent mechanics and rhythm for game shots.

**Equipment Needed:** 1 ball per player, 1 hoop.

**Instructions:**
1. Start 3–5 feet from the basket.
2. Make 15–25 shots with full focus on mechanics.
3. Step back to mid-range spots and repeat.

**Coaching Tips:** Maintain consistent footwork and aim for soft touch off the rim.
