# Adults Passing Drill

## Purpose
Enhance passing precision, timing, and decision-making in competitive scenarios.

## Equipment Needed
- Basketballs
- Cones
- 3-4 players per group

## Instructions
1. Set up a half-court passing circuit with cones.
2. Move the ball around the perimeter with quick reversals.
3. Add cutters and require lead passes to moving targets.
4. Rotate roles after each sequence.

## Coaching Tips
- Deliver passes to where teammates are going.
- Keep turnover count low under pressure.
- Communicate every pass and cut.
