# Adults Defense Drill

## Purpose
Build defensive endurance, closeout technique, and team help principles.

## Equipment Needed
- Cones
- Court space
- 2+ players

## Instructions
1. Start with a closeout from help position to an offensive cone.
2. Transition into defensive slides to cut off baseline and middle.
3. Recover to help position and repeat continuously.
4. Progress to live 1-on-1 or 2-on-2 from the same setup.

## Coaching Tips
- Chop steps on closeout and contest without fouling.
- Keep active hands and balanced stance.
- Communicate switches and help coverage loudly.
