# Partner Chest Pass Drill

**Purpose:** Build crisp, game-speed passing and communication.

**Equipment Needed:** 1 ball per pair.

**Instructions:**
1. Partners stand 10–15 feet apart in athletic stance.
2. Deliver firm passes with a step and quick release.
3. Complete 25–30 passes, then add a pass fake before each pass.

**Coaching Tips:** Hit the target quickly and communicate on every rep.
