# Zigzag Dribble Drill

**Purpose:** Develop advanced ball control, change of pace, and game-like moves.

**Equipment Needed:** 6–8 cones, 1 ball per player.

**Instructions:**
1. Set cones 7–10 feet apart from baseline to the opposite free-throw line.
2. Dribble to each cone and use a crossover, between-the-legs, or behind-the-back move.
3. Complete three trips each hand, finishing the last trip at full speed.

**Coaching Tips:** Keep the ball tight to the body and explode out of each change.
