# Teens Defense Drill

## Purpose
Develop on-ball defense, lateral quickness, and recovery effort.

## Equipment Needed
- Cones
- Court lane area

## Instructions
1. Set start and finish cones 12-15 feet apart.
2. Player starts in defensive stance at start cone.
3. Slide to finish cone, then sprint back and recover to stance.
4. Complete in short high-intensity sets.

## Coaching Tips
- Stay low and keep chest up.
- Sprint-recover with urgency.
- Focus on footwork discipline when tired.
