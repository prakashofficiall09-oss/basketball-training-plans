# Teens Shooting Drill

## Purpose
Improve shot consistency from mid-range and three-point spots.

## Equipment Needed
- Basketballs
- Hoop
- Spot markers

## Instructions
1. Set 5 shooting spots around the half court.
2. Players take 5 shots from each spot.
3. Track makes and move to next spot.
4. Repeat for 2-3 rounds.

## Coaching Tips
- Focus on repeatable shooting mechanics.
- Encourage quick foot preparation.
- Track percentages to measure progress.
