# Partner Chest Pass Drill

**Purpose:** Improve passing accuracy, timing, and readiness.

**Equipment Needed:** 1 ball per pair.

**Instructions:**
1. Partners stand 8–12 feet apart in athletic stance.
2. Pass with a quick step and snap of the wrists.
3. Complete 20 passes, then add one pivot before each pass.

**Coaching Tips:** Pass on time and keep hands ready as a target.
