# Kids Dribbling Drill

## Purpose
Build basic ball control, hand coordination, and confidence while dribbling.

## Equipment Needed
- 1 basketball per player
- Cones (4-6)
- Flat practice space

## Instructions
1. Set cones in a straight line with enough spacing for easy movement.
2. Players dribble with their dominant hand through the cones.
3. Repeat using the non-dominant hand.
4. Finish with a short timed round for fun.

## Coaching Tips
- Encourage players to keep their eyes up.
- Focus on control before speed.
- Use positive feedback to build confidence.
