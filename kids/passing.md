# Kids Passing Drill

## Purpose
Improve passing accuracy, timing, and teamwork.

## Equipment Needed
- Basketballs
- Cones or markers

## Instructions
1. Pair players and space them 8-10 feet apart.
2. Practice chest passes for 60 seconds.
3. Switch to bounce passes for 60 seconds.
4. Add movement by having pairs shuffle while passing.

## Coaching Tips
- Emphasize stepping toward the target.
- Encourage strong two-hand passes.
- Remind players to call for the ball.
