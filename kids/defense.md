# Kids Defense Drill

## Purpose
Teach defensive stance, footwork, and staying in front of an opponent.

## Equipment Needed
- Cones
- Flat court area

## Instructions
1. Mark a short lane with cones.
2. Players start in defensive stance.
3. On coach signal, players slide laterally from cone to cone.
4. Repeat for multiple short rounds with rest breaks.

## Coaching Tips
- Keep hips low and hands active.
- No crossing feet during slides.
- Use short rounds to maintain good form.
