# Partner Chest Pass Drill

**Purpose:** Improve passing accuracy and timing.

**Equipment Needed:** 1 ball per pair.

**Instructions:**
1. Partners stand 5–8 feet apart in athletic stance.
2. Pass with a quick step toward the target.
3. Complete 10–15 passes, then switch to bounce passes.

**Coaching Tips:** Aim for the receiver’s chest and stay ready to catch.
