# Partner Chest Pass Drill

**Purpose:** Improve passing accuracy and timing.

**Equipment Needed:** 1 ball per pair.

**Instructions:**
1. Partners stand 8–12 feet apart in athletic stance.
2. Pass with a quick step toward the target.
3. Complete 20 passes, then increase distance slightly.

**Coaching Tips:** Snap wrists, aim for the receiver’s chest, and stay ready to catch.
