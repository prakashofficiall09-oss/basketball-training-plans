# Kids Shooting Drill

## Purpose
Develop shooting form and touch close to the basket.

## Equipment Needed
- Basketballs
- Hoop
- Floor markers (optional)

## Instructions
1. Place players in a short line near the hoop.
2. Practice one-hand form shooting close to the rim.
3. Add guide hand and repeat with full form.
4. Rotate quickly so each player gets multiple attempts.

## Coaching Tips
- Teach balanced stance and follow-through.
- Keep shooting range short at first.
- Reinforce proper mechanics over makes/misses.
